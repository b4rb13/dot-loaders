## Approved first delivery decisions — Q1–Q3

1. **Complete public v1 with incremental private milestones.** Public stable v1 includes the full agreed free native/React catalog and source-informed motion, supported SSR/CSP, customization, docs/playground and governance. Earlier private artifacts and a protected alpha prove increments without labeling a partial implementation complete. No calendar release date until costs and prerequisites are known.
2. **Release blockers versus later growth.** Accepted functionality, content/focus identity, interruption, package/type/CSS integrity, source rights, actual compatibility/accessibility, resource/cleanup gates and accurate docs are release obligations. Honor the existing narrow numerical-exception policy; no blanket waiver. Extra framework/chart/grid adapters, additional future motion collections, paid templates/tools/commerce and adoption growth are later work. Required tooling/hardware/identity/protection evidence can remain an explicitly unverified implementation prerequisite; it cannot be declared passed or silently removed.
3. **First implementation milestone: foundation plus one complete native flow.** Establish reproducible pinned Bun/Turborepo boundaries, scoped provenance/extraction plan, compiled selective exports/declarations/CSS and an external packed native consumer. Prove SkeletonBlock inside LoadingRegion through pending/ready/refresh/replacement, immediate outcome recipe, interruption, preferences/four-second window, static server fallback, documented CSP delivery and cleanup. Establish the approved lifecycle/animation contract and a small custom-definition conformance fixture early. Keep renderer choices subject to their approved measurement work. This milestone is private, may span sessions, and does not claim the full catalog, React/site implementation or all shipping gates. No copying from m37 until explicitly authorized by the scoped implementation work; no publishing/deploying in this planning ticket.
