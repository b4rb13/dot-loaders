A region is as tall as its content once it has handed over, and while it refreshes. The placeholder used to keep its space after `ready`, so a placeholder taller than the content left blank space for good, including on error states (`state="ready"` with `timing.duration: 0`). The placeholder still keeps its place through the handoff, which reads it, and the region's presentation now goes from `pending` straight to `handoff`.
