Decorations are inert and hidden from assistive technology. The actual application content is the accessible source of truth. Regions manage busy/hidden/inert presentation and preserve focus; if replacement would hide focused content, the established focus-safe behavior takes priority. Your application supplies localized status/error announcements and request outcomes.

Persistent visitor pause, reduced motion, forced colors, hidden/offscreen/zero-size conditions and resource limits can make decoration static or reveal immediately. Moving activity is bounded by the inclusive four-second region window; it does not restart indefinitely while a request remains pending.
