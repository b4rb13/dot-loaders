Dotquilt's motion is slow and short by design, and every part of it can be stilled.

## Reduced motion

When a visitor asks their system for reduced motion, loaders show their still picture with no glow or breathing, and the handoff swaps at once. You don't need to do anything.

## Pausing

Set `motion="paused"` on any element, and every loader inside it is still. To give visitors a pause control for the whole page, set it on `<html>`:

```ts
pauseButton.addEventListener('click', () => {
  const paused = document.documentElement.getAttribute('motion') === 'paused';
  document.documentElement.setAttribute('motion', paused ? 'system' : 'paused');
});
```

## The four-second limit

Automatic motion never runs longer than four seconds. A loader's idle stops four seconds into a load and rests, so a long load never animates indefinitely. The handoff still plays when the content arrives, however long the load took: it lasts about a second. A handoff you ask for that is longer than four seconds is skipped.

## Focus

- **Pending content is out of reach:** while a region is pending, its content is inert and hidden from assistive technology.
- **Focus is kept:** if focus is inside content that becomes pending, it moves to the region. When the content is ready, focus is not moved back; your page decides.
- **A focused handoff swaps at once,** so nothing moves under the keyboard.

## What screen readers hear

- **The marks are silent.** Every loader is hidden from assistive technology with `aria-hidden`, and so is the placeholder layer.
- **Pending content is skipped.** While a region is `pending`, its content is `inert`, hidden with `aria-hidden`, and marked `aria-busy="true"`, so screen readers don't read half-loaded content.
- **Refreshing content stays readable.** While a region is `refreshing`, its content stays visible and usable, and is marked `aria-busy="true"` until it's ready.
- **Ready content reads as usual.** When the region is `ready`, `aria-busy` becomes `false`. Nothing is announced on its own: see below.

## Announcements

Loaders are decoration: they don't announce anything. Label every region, so people know what is loading, and announce loading and errors in your own words, for example with a `role="status"` element outside the region.

## Forced colours

In Windows high-contrast mode the marks are replaced by a dotted outline in the system text colour.
