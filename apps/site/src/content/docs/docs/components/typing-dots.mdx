TypingDots show that a reply is on its way. A slow wave passes through the dots; when the message arrives, it takes their place.

## Usage

Use TypingDots as a region's placeholder, inside the same bubble your message will fill.

<Tabs syncKey="framework">
  <TabItem label="React">
    <Code code={replyTsx.replaceAll("'../data'", "'./data'")} lang="tsx" title="AssistantReply.tsx" />
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Native">
    <Code code={replyHtml} lang="html" title="index.html" />
    <Code code={replyTs.replaceAll("'../data'", "'./data'")} lang="ts" title="reply.ts" />
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

TypingDots are decorative: they have no state of their own. On their own, outside a region, they keep their wave for four seconds and then rest.

## Options

| Attribute | React | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `count` | `count` | number | `3` | How many dots, up to 8. |
| `mark` | `mark` | `dot` \| `square` \| `dash` | `dot` | The shape of each dot. |
| `speed` | `speed` | number | `1` | How fast the wave moves. |
| `motion` | `motion` | `system` \| `paused` | `system` | `system` follows the visitor's reduced-motion setting; `paused` stills the dots. |

The dots are 8 px, 6 px apart. Change them with `--dotquilt-dot-size` and `--dotquilt-dot-gap`.
