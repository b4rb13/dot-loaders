TypingDots, Dotquilt's typing indicator, show that a reply is on its way. A slow wave passes through the dots; when the message arrives, it takes their place.

## Usage

Use TypingDots as a region's placeholder, inside the same bubble your message will fill.

TypingDots are decorative: they have no state of their own. On their own, outside a region, they keep their wave for four seconds and then rest.

## Accessibility

- **The dots are decoration.** They are hidden from assistive technology, so screen readers don't read them.
- **Label the region,** for example `aria-label="Assistant reply"`, so people know what is on its way. While it is pending, the message inside is skipped.
- **Announce the reply in your own words** if people need to hear it arrive, for example with a `role="log"` or `role="status"` element for the conversation. See [What screen readers hear](/docs/guides/motion/#what-screen-readers-hear).

## Options

| Attribute | Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `count` | `count` | number | `3` | How many dots, up to 8. |
| `mark` | `mark` | `dot` \| `square` \| `dash` | `dot` | The shape of each dot. |
| `speed` | `speed` | number | `1` | How fast the wave moves. |
| `motion` | `motion` | `system` \| `paused` | `system` | `system` follows the visitor's reduced-motion setting; `paused` stills the dots. |

The dots are 8 px, 6 px apart. Change them with `--dotquilt-dot-size` and `--dotquilt-dot-gap`.
