Dotquilt is developed in the open, so most conversations happen on GitHub, where everyone who has the same question can find the answer.

## Bugs

Open an issue at [github.com/b4rb13/dot-loaders/issues](https://github.com/b4rb13/dot-loaders/issues). It helps to include:

- the Dotquilt version (`npm ls dotquilt`) and, for React, `@dotquilt/react`;
- your framework and its version, and your browser;
- what you expected to happen, and what happened instead;
- a small example that shows it, such as a link to a sandbox or a few lines of code.

## Questions and ideas

Start with the [docs](/docs/), or ask your coding assistant with the [setup prompt](/docs/ai-setup/). If the answer isn't there, open an issue with your question or idea; a question that comes up often becomes a page in the docs.

## Security problems

Please don't post the details of a security problem in a public issue. Open an issue that says only that you have a security report to make, without the details, and a maintainer will reply with a private way to send them.

## Pull requests

Pull requests are welcome. [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/b4rb13/dot-loaders/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) explains the setup, the checks every change runs, and how releases are made.
