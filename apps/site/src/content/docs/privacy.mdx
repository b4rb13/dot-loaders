Effective 21 November 2026.

dotquilt.dev is a documentation website. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no forms, sets no cookies, and shows no advertising. Here is everything that happens with your data when you visit.

## Hosting

The site is hosted by [Vercel](https://vercel.com). Like any web server, Vercel receives your IP address and the details of each request (such as the page, your browser and the time) to deliver the page and protect the site against abuse. See [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Analytics

The site uses [Vercel Web Analytics](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy) to count visits. It sets no cookies. Instead, a visitor is recognised for one day by a hash made from the request, which resets every day, so a visit can't be followed across days or across other websites. It records aggregated statistics only: which pages are visited, where visitors came from, and their country, browser, operating system and kind of device.

## Fonts

The site's fonts come from [Google Fonts](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy). Your browser downloads them from Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details when it does.

## Stored on your device

Two small values stay in your own browser and are never sent anywhere:

- your light or dark theme choice, in local storage, if you pick one;
- whether the header's opening animation has played during this visit, in session storage, which your browser clears when you close the tab.

The playground keeps your settings in the page's address, not on any server. The copy buttons use your clipboard only when you click them.

## Other sites

The GitHub star count in the header is read when the site is built, so your browser makes no request to GitHub until you follow a link. Links to GitHub and npm are covered by those sites' own policies.
